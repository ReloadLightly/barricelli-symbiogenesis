# Primary-source research notes

Read versions and retrieval hashes are in `related-retrieval.json`. These notes summarize methods relevant to this repository; their systems are not interchangeable with Barricelli's. No source figures or complete third-party programs are copied into the artwork.

| Source actually inspected | Relevant mechanism / finding | Method adopted or caution |
|---|---|---|
| [ASAL, arXiv2412.17799v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.17799v2), Kumar et al. | Foundation-model embeddings support target search, temporal novelty and illumination across ALife substrates. CLIP/DINOv2 provide representations; search occurs between simulations. | Compare exploration policies with a fixed simulator and renderer. Here descriptors are numerical and Codex proposes configurations conversationally; no CLIP evaluation or ASAL replication was executed. Representation-space novelty is not inherited biological novelty. |
| [ASAL++, arXiv2509.22447v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.22447v1), Baid et al. | Gemma-3 proposes new targets from simulation history; CLIP and Sep-CMA-ES optimize Lenia. EST targets one new prompt; ETT retains a sequence. Warm starts and model costs matter. | Log feedback and hypotheses between batches. Our finite configuration proposals have no target image, no per-cell model and no autonomous target-generation service. A shared conversational context limits independence of repeated model-assisted searches. |
| [Computational Life, arXiv2406.19108v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.19108v2), Agüera y Arcas et al. | Interacting self-modifying programs in several languages generate self-replicators without an explicit fitness landscape; behavior depends on the computational substrate. | Replay and functional recovery matter more than appearance. BFF tapes and integer-displacement worlds have different mechanisms and should not share an untested organism detector. |
| [BFF follow-up, arXiv2607.01483v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.01483v1), “BFF: Simple explanations for complex phenomena” | A repeated noisy-partner assay detects functional copying; tuned random mutation can find replicators faster than interactions, while interactions support diffusion. Merger interventions investigate composition. | Separate first appearance, viable repeated reproduction and population spread. Use matched random baselines and component interventions. Do not infer discovery efficiency from takeover pictures. Their detector also discusses periodic-shift false positives. |
| [Distributed replication, arXiv2508.08047v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.08047v1), Hintze and Bohm | Outlier CA ancestry reconstruction identifies offspring with continuing descendants, including disjoint cooperating clusters and forking/merging causal pathways. | Track copied sources and enabling partners. Our scoped events record local dependencies; they do not yet reconstruct complete collective ancestry. The conservative contiguous-footprint assay can miss distributed reproductive organization. |
| [Programmable Cellular Automata, arXiv2609.06102v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.06102v2), Khalifa et al. | Inspectable code represents local/global cellular functions; LLM-assisted and evolutionary search address procedural-content tasks. | Keep research rule variants small, named and inspectable. Do not transplant task rewards or claim a reproduction detector from a content-generation result. We inspect collision formulas rather than evolve arbitrary code in this milestone. |
| [Lenia, arXiv1812.05433v3](https://arxiv.org/html/1812.05433v3), Bert Wang-Chak Chan | Continuous cellular dynamics produce a varied morphology vocabulary; taxonomy, parameter exploration and stability/motion measurements accompany visual examples. | Keep a replayable morphology atlas and measure persistence and motion independently. Lenia is context, not a replacement substrate or an engine executed here. |
| [Karl Sims, Genetic Images](https://www.karlsims.com/genetic-images.html),1993 installation and artist's essay | Visitors select mathematical image genotypes, steering an aesthetic evolutionary process across generations. | Explicitly label curation as externally imposed aesthetic selection and retain the unselected collection. Our images arise from saved dynamical histories rather than direct image functions or hidden target pictures. |

## Existing Barricelli replications

[The March2026 SymBa workshop report, arXiv2603.08463v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.08463v1), was inspected in HTML, especially sections5–6. It already reconstructs1D systems, discusses2D and Boolean extensions, reports information diagnostics, and presents DNA-inspired norms. These are existing replications and extensions. Its stable-sequence and robustness proposals are relevant to the causal-identification problem; this repository does not claim to originate Barricelli reconstruction or motif-based analysis.

The [SymBa repository](https://github.com/JELAshford/symba-alice-2026) was inspected at commit `2b6fc9375553ccdb6c955f271889a762c1624039`: `basic_barricelli.py` gathers sets of candidate values before resolving collisions, unlike the retrieved Galloway sketch's sequential erased-target reuse. Its combined environment performs norm lookups in sliced regional states; this repository's declared research mixture searches the whole old ring across rule boundaries. These are code differences, not correctness judgments. No code from this repository was copied.

[The July2026 GECCO Companion paper](https://doi.org/10.1145/3795101.3814707), “Symbiogenesis: from Barricelli's Legacy to Collective Intelligence,” is a separate conference version. The DOI publisher request returned403. Bibliographic metadata and abstract were inspected; the full conference paper was not read. Its full experimental coverage and possible overlap have not been excluded.

[The2026 translation](https://www.tim-taylor.com/papers/barricelli2026numerical.pdf) and [Taylor–Bianchi commentary](https://www.tim-taylor.com/papers/taylor2026commentary.pdf) ground the1954 profiles. The1957 paper is a distinct account, not an English republication of1954. [Galloway's published sketch](https://cultureandcommunication.org/galloway/Barricelli/Barricelli.pde) and [retrospective](https://cultureandcommunication.org/galloway/ephemera-pt-3-barricelli) ground the artistic reconstruction. [Chapter5](https://www.tim-taylor.com/selfrepbook/web/chapter-5.html), Figure5.4, credits a Galloway reconstruction as the historical context for the example image.
